# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Overwhelmed Browser
- **Primary metric:** Time-to-Play (TTP) - the median time from opening the app to start watching content
- **Moment of misery:** Choice overload causes users to leave without watching anything, rewatch the same few shows, or cancel. One lapsed subscriber called the catalog "a warehouse."
- **Guardrail:** Playback Start Failure Rate, based on % of playbacks that fail or have stalling >10 secs or redirect to home page

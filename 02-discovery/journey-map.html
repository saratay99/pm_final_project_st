StreamLine Spotlight: The Overwhelmed Browser

From a 20-minute scroll to trusted discovery, in four stages
Strategy: Restore brand authority and win back users through human-led curation, not mass-market scale.

Persona

Loyal, long-time subscriber who opens the app most evenings hoping to unwind.

Goal

Quickly find something new for tonight's mood, without it feeling like work.

Today's workaround

Scrolls ~20 min through ~15,000 titles, gives up, and puts on a DVD or re-watches comfort shows. Mutes the TV to escape loud trailers.
Emotional arc
1

Arrive

"What's your mood tonight?"
User Action

Taps "Quiet Sunday" on opening the app → catalog narrows instantly to her mood.

Internal State
Calm & in control

One small choice instead of 15,000 → she feels calm, not anxious.

Pain Point Addressed

The 20-minute scroll → she starts with direction, not a wall of titles.

UXR-01
2

Discover

The Spotlight Rail
User Action

Browses a handful of hand-picked titles → sees hidden gems, not franchise repeats.

Internal State
Curious & trusting

Human curation feels personal → trust in recommendations comes back.

Pain Point Addressed

Choice overload, no mood browsing → someone "tells her what's good tonight."

UXR-09 · UXR-12
3

Decide

Curator context, quiet preview
User Action

Reads a short curator's note on why it fits → commits without second-guessing.

Internal State
Confident & unhurried

Previews play muted until she opts in → she browses in peace.

Pain Point Addressed

Loud autoplay, failed descriptive search → no muting the TV, no dead ends.

Watch & Return

From one night to a ritual
User Action

Finishes something new she enjoyed → StreamLine becomes where her evening starts.

Internal State
Satisfied & loyal

A weekly digest brings fresh picks → she looks forward to discovering.

Pain Point Addressed

Comfort loop, pull toward competitors → she explores again and stays subscribed.

UXR-03 · UXR-04

Competitive Advantages Over the Manual Workaround

1. Speed with novelty Gets her to something new in a few curated choices.
vs. DVD fallback: ~20 minutes, ends with titles she already owns.
2. Use of the full catalog Curation brings the deeper catalog back into her viewing.
vs. Comfort loop: 4 titles out of 15,000, an easily replaced subscription.
3. Human trust, in-app Delivers the curated, trusted experience curation-focused rivals win on today.
vs. Competitor's two hand-picked films a week that drew Tom away (UXR-04).
Sources: StreamLine user research (UXR-01–12) and bug reports (BUG-1042–1121). Benefits shown are target outcomes to validate, not measured results.

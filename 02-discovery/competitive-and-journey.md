# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The Overwhelmed Browser
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Quickly find something new that suits her mood tonight, without it feeling like work.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She scrolls for about 20 minutes through roughly 15,000 titles, closes the app without watching anything, and ends up putting on a DVD (UXR-01). If she tries to describe what she wants, search fails. A query like "slow quiet French drama" returns a Fast & Furious film (BUG-1080, UXR-07). There's also no way to browse by mood, and loud autoplay trailers interrupt her while she's still deciding (BUG-1077). Over time she stops exploring and just re-watches the same few comfort shows (UXR-03).
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** The "DVD Fallback"
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** She opens StreamLine wanting to watch something that evening.
She scrolls the home rows for about 20 minutes.
She tries to describe what she wants in search, and gets irrelevant results.
She closes the app without playing anything.
She picks a DVD from her own collection.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** She spends 20 minutes and still ends up choosing from a small personal collection of titles she already owns. The effort goes up and the variety of what she watches goes down.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "Honestly I just re-watch the same four comfort shows. Discovering anything new feels like a chore, so I don't."
